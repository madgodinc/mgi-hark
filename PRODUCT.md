# Hark (mgi-hark)

register: product
platform: web (Tauri desktop, Windows)

## What it is

Live captions for people with severe hearing loss. Hark listens to one program's audio (Discord, Steam, TeamSpeak) or to the whole system, turns Russian speech into text on the user's own machine, and shows it in a transparent overlay on top of the game.

## Who uses it

Deaf and hard-of-hearing players who want to play with friends in voice chat. Some of them read Russian with difficulty, so the text must be large, plain and adjustable. Many will be sent the installer by a friend and set it up alone.

## Scene

A player in a dim room at night, a game running full screen in borderless mode. They open the settings window for a minute before the match, pick Discord, make the text big enough, then forget the window exists. The overlay is what they look at for hours.

## Principles

- The overlay is the product. The settings window serves it and previews it exactly.
- Readability over style: every look setting shows its effect live.
- No accounts, no cloud: speech never leaves the computer.
- Nothing may require reading long instructions.
